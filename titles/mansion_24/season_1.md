Amrutha, an investigative journalist, searches for her missing father Kalidas, an archaeologist accused of absconding with valuable artifacts and branded a traitor. He was last seen at an old, reportedly haunted mansion where it's believed that no one who enters ever returns alive. Amrutha ventures there to uncover the truth and clear his name.
